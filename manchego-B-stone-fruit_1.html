the short version

We started at a market with one plate and a folding table.

Manchego cooks Dutch-style mini pancakes to order. Not warmed. Not stacked in a chafing dish an hour before service. Cooked, on a cast-iron plate, in front of the person eating them.

That turned out to matter more than the recipe. The cart pulls people out of their seats, gives shy guests something to do with their hands, and fills the awkward hour between dinner and dancing better than any plated dessert we have seen.

Now there are three carts, a parfait build, and a grazing table we assemble on site over ninety minutes.
