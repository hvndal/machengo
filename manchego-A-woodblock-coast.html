01 / Who shows up

We are two carts, one van, and a batter recipe we have not changed since the first farmers market.

Manchego started as a weekend stall in Kelowna, cooking Dutch-style mini pancakes on a cast-iron plate because a friend's wedding had a dessert gap and no budget for a cake table.

The format stuck. Guests eat standing up, they talk to whoever is holding the tongs, and nobody sits through a plated dessert course. It is catering that behaves like a bar.

Today we run pancakes, parfaits, and grazing across the Okanagan and down to the coast. Same cart, same plate, more sauces.

Everything is cooked live. Nothing arrives pre-plated in a hotel pan.
