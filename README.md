## Brand Story & Services
MANCH EGO (*"Crafted with Andalusian Soul"*) brings authentic mini-pancakes, layered parfaits, and artisanal grazing boards to weddings, private parties, corporate gatherings, and community events across **British Columbia**.

### Creative Directions
This showcase deck presents the top three creative directions:
1. **Route D — Kelowna / Vancouver Pop (`manchego-D-vancouver-pop.html`)**: Client top favorite featuring handcrafted illustration, playful typography, and real-time interactive event booking.
2. **Route B — Stone Fruit (`manchego-B-stone-fruit.html`)**: Variable serif warmth inspired by Okanagan orchards.
3. **Route A — Woodblock Coast (`manchego-A-woodblock-coast.html`)**: High-impact artisanal craft derived from routed cart lettering.
